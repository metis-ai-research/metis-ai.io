# metis-ai.io home (src/App.js)

Scope: the single-page public site. Mode: Persuade. Primary visitor: a business weighing a partnership. Action: start a conversation (info@metis-ai.io). Proof: the live products (arotaro, Blinc) and Metis OS (screenshot plus its real pillars). Constraints: EN and JA, keep the logo and blue brand, dark theme, no invented metrics or clients, Microsoft for Startups dropped.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard played straight: a premium dark tech-studio page held to Stripe's craft and motion bar. It refuses the current glow-and-gradient-text navy template; polish comes from type, real product UI and orchestrated motion, not decoration.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black blue ground (#05070F family) with ink-white text and Metis blue (#1A3EF3 to #5C86F7) as the single accent. One slow, drifting brand-blue light field lives behind the hero only. Surfaces are hairline-bordered panels with real offset shadows. Mona Sans for Latin, Murecho for Japanese, mono only inside product UI. Icons are authored SVG.
STORY: Within one screen, the visitor understands that this is a four-person team that builds and runs live products with AI employees. The product demos prove it (arotaro chat, Blinc tap-to-barcode, the Metis OS approval flow), then come the principles and the team, and the visitor ends by emailing or copying info@metis-ai.io.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav (logo, four links, EN/JA, Contact). A centered two-line headline at about 88px with subcopy and two buttons (Start a conversation, primary blue; See our products). The Metis OS screenshot rises in perspective below the fold line and flattens as you scroll. Signature motion: a staggered headline rise, then the screenshot landing.
FORM: The category standard (canon card), chosen by the user after seeing four first-viewport sketches; seed:1f26a705d16bd692 (re-roll 1, canon chosen). Craft benchmark: Stripe.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
